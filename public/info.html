Drew Bordeaux

Info

Drew Bordeaux is a singer-songwriter, record producer and creative director.

He started on violin at five and has played more than a thousand shows since, sharing bills with Daughtry and the Gin Blossoms and opening at The Paramount.

His albums are Impulse / Instinct (2021) and Dust Between Galaxies (2026), the second on his own label, Sudden Flight Records. He produced Becki Davis’s Lost & Found, which reached #7 on the Folk Alliance International albums chart.

He has made creative direction and photography for Grammy-winning artists, including Nicole Zuraitis, and his work has appeared in Vogue, Billboard and DownBeat. He is a voting member of the Recording Academy.
